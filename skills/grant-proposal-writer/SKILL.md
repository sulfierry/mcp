# Grant Proposal Writer

## Use this skill when

- Writing a research grant proposal (NSF, NIH, FAPESP, CAPES, CNPq)
- Drafting Specific Aims or Research Plan sections
- Preparing budget justifications
- Writing broader impacts / relevance statements
- Structuring a project narrative for funding agencies
- Responding to reviewer comments on a resubmission

## Instructions

### Universal Grant Structure

```
1. SPECIFIC AIMS / OBJECTIVES (1 page)
   - Opening paragraph: significance + knowledge gap
   - "The long-term goal of this project is..."
   - "The objective of this proposal is..."
   - Aim 1: [Specific, measurable, achievable]
   - Aim 2: [With clear deliverables]
   - Aim 3: [With timeline]
   - Impact paragraph: what happens if you succeed

2. SIGNIFICANCE / BACKGROUND (2-3 pages)
   - Why this problem matters
   - Current state of knowledge
   - What gap this fills
   - Preliminary data (if available)

3. RESEARCH STRATEGY / METHODS (5-10 pages)
   - Aim 1: Rationale → Approach → Expected outcomes → Pitfalls & alternatives
   - Aim 2: Same structure
   - Aim 3: Same structure
   - Timeline (Gantt chart)

4. BROADER IMPACTS / RELEVANCE
   - Societal benefit
   - Training and mentoring plan
   - Dissemination plan
   - Diversity and inclusion

5. BUDGET & JUSTIFICATION
   - Personnel
   - Equipment
   - Travel
   - Other costs
```

### Brazilian Funding Agencies

#### FAPESP (São Paulo Research Foundation)
```
Modalidades:
- Auxílio Regular: R$ 300K, 24 meses
- Projeto Temático: R$ 2-5M, 60 meses
- PIPE (empresas): Fase 1 R$ 300K, Fase 2 R$ 1M

Estrutura do Projeto:
1. Resumo (pt + en)
2. Introdução e Justificativa
3. Objetivos (geral e específicos)
4. Plano de Trabalho e Cronograma
5. Material e Métodos
6. Forma de Análise dos Resultados
7. Disseminação e Avaliação
8. Orçamento Detalhado
9. Referências Bibliográficas
```

#### CAPES / CNPq
```
Estrutura do Projeto:
1. Identificação da Proposta
2. Área e Subárea do Conhecimento
3. Resumo (pt + en)
4. Introdução e Justificativa
5. Objetivos
6. Metodologia
7. Cronograma de Execução
8. Orçamento
9. Resultados Esperados
10. Referências
11. Currículo Lattes atualizado
```

### Specific Aims Template (NIH/NSF style)

```
[Opening hook — 2 sentences on the problem and its significance]

[Knowledge gap paragraph — what we don't know and why it matters]

The long-term goal of this research is to [broad vision]. The objective 
of this proposal is to [specific, achievable goal within the grant period]. 
Our central hypothesis is that [testable statement], based on [preliminary 
data / published evidence].

Our rationale is that [why this approach will work], and upon completion, 
[what will be possible that isn't possible now].

We will test our central hypothesis by pursuing the following specific aims:

Aim 1: [Verb] [what] [how] [expected outcome]
  Working hypothesis: [specific prediction]

Aim 2: [Verb] [what] [how] [expected outcome]  
  Working hypothesis: [specific prediction]

Aim 3: [Verb] [what] [how] [expected outcome]
  Working hypothesis: [specific prediction]

[Impact paragraph — innovation, expected outcomes, and significance 
 even if some aims are not fully achieved]
```

### Budget Justification Tips

```
Personnel (≤80% of total for most agencies):
- "Dr. X (PI) will dedicate 20% effort to project oversight and data analysis"
- "One PhD student (full-time) will conduct experiments in Aims 1-2"

Equipment (>R$10K or >$5K):
- Justify why existing equipment is insufficient
- Get competing quotes

Travel:
- "PI will attend [conference] to present results (Aim 3, Year 2)"
- "Collaboration visit to [lab] for [specific technique] (2 weeks, Year 1)"

Publication costs:
- "Open access publication fees for 3 expected publications"
```
