# Elite Cover Letter Craft

## When to use
- Submitting to Nature/Science/Cell family, NEJM, Lancet, PNAS, eLife, or comparable flagship
- Sending presubmission inquiry (Nature, eLife, Cell allow; Science does not formally)
- Cascading transfer to sister journal after rejection

## Length rule

| Venue | Max | Typical |
|-------|-----|---------|
| Nature / Science / Cell | 1 page | 300-400 words |
| Society journals | 1-2 pages | 400-600 words |
| Presubmission inquiry | 300 words | abstract + pitch |

Editors read hundreds weekly. 30-second scan is realistic. Front-load.

## Elite 4-paragraph structure (300-400 w)

### P1 — Headline (50-70 w)
- ONE sentence: what you did + what you found.
- Second sentence: why this changes field's understanding.
- No context paragraph. No preamble.

Example opener:
> "We report the cryo-EM structure of X in complex with Y, resolving the longstanding mystery of Z mechanism."

### P2 — Context + novelty (80-100 w)
- What field previously believed / could do.
- What was the critical barrier.
- What your approach overcomes.
- Explicit comparison: "Unlike prior studies that [limitation], we [advance]."

### P3 — Evidence highlights (80-100 w)
- 3-4 concrete quantitative results (with units).
- Phrase as claims + numbers, not methods.
- e.g., "binding affinity improves 100-fold (K_D = 12 nM vs 1.2 µM for wild-type)."

### P4 — Fit + significance + logistics (70-100 w)
- Why THIS journal (audience, scope, companion work).
- Broader implication (discipline-level).
- Logistics: exclusive submission, preprint status (bioRxiv DOI), suggested/opposed reviewers, related papers under review/published.
- Thank + sign off.

## Presubmission inquiry (Nature family)

300 words max. Format:

```
Subject: Presubmission inquiry — [Working title]

Dear Editor,

Paragraph 1 (60 w): One-sentence finding + why interesting to broad readership.

Paragraph 2 (60 w): What's new vs existing literature (cite 2-3 recent refs).

Paragraph 3 (60 w): 3 quantitative results.

Paragraph 4 (60 w): Fit statement + related work in pipeline (if any) + preprint link (if posted).

[Abstract attached / Figure 1 attached]

Sincerely,
[Name, affiliation, ORCID]
```

Response time: 1-2 weeks. Outcomes: "please submit" (~30%), "not a good fit" (~60%), "reformulate and try" (~10%).

## Editor targeting

### Find the right editor
- Nature family: subject editors listed on journal "about" page. Match your topic to their stated interests.
- Cell: generalist editors handle broad submissions; some family journals (Cell Reports) have subject editors.
- Science: no editor selection — handled by board.
- eLife: "deputy editor" by field, selects reviewing editor.

### Personalize P4
Mention editor's recent editorials or a paper they handled — shows you've read the journal, not spamming.

Do NOT: attempt editor "connections" if none exist. Backfires.

## Suggested / opposed reviewers

Most top journals request:
- **Suggested** (3-6): neutral experts. Name + affiliation + email + reason (1 line).
- **Opposed** (1-3): direct competitors with conflict (competitor, scooped, advisor/ex-advisor, bad-faith prior reviews). Name + reason.

Avoid: close collaborators (past 3-5 years), same department, co-authors. Editor will exclude them.

## Novelty claim — credibly framed

Top-tier editors are novelty-suspicious. Formulate novelty with evidence:

- **Specific**: "the first observation of X in [system]" not "novel approach"
- **Comparative**: "vs Smith et al. 2023 who reported Y, we extend to Z" — requires ref.
- **Measurable**: "10-fold improvement over state-of-the-art [citation]"
- **Conceptual**: "paradigm shift" language only if you genuinely have one — editors detect bluster instantly.

## Preprints + priority

If paper is already on bioRxiv/arXiv/medRxiv:
- State: "This manuscript is available as a preprint at [DOI]."
- Don't apologize. Most top venues now neutral or positive on preprints.
- Add: "posted on [date]" for priority record.

Nature, Cell, Science, eLife, PNAS all allow preprints. NEJM disallows for ongoing clinical trials.

## Exclusive submission + prior consideration

Declare:
- Not under consideration elsewhere (ethics)
- If transferred from another journal: name source + reviewer reports carried

Failing to disclose transfer = misconduct if discovered.

## Multi-paper submissions (companion papers)

If submitting 2-3 linked papers:
- One cover letter describes package
- Explain dependencies: "Paper A establishes method X used in Papers B,C"
- Editors often desk-reject without the package framing — treat it as atomic.

## Tone

- **Confident, not arrogant**: state what you did, not what's "amazing".
- **Specific, not vague**: numbers, names, timeframes.
- **Honest, not hedging**: if limitation exists, don't hide; frame as scope boundary.
- **Formal, not stiff**: "we" OK; contractions avoid.

## Common auto-rejects

- "We believe this paper would be of interest to..." (weak)
- "Novel/unprecedented/paradigm-shifting" without evidence
- ≥2 pages
- Starts with CV humble-brag
- Claims of general medical breakthrough from cell-line study
- "Please consider" (weak close)
- Suggested reviewers all from own group
- Attachment confusion (abstract not provided)

- **Grammarly**, **Hemingway** — linting.

## Template: 1-shot Nature-grade cover letter

```
Dear [Editor Name],

We submit our manuscript, "[Title]", for consideration at [Journal].
We report [one-sentence finding], providing [broad implication in one
clause].

The field has previously held that [prior belief]. While [cite
representative work] advanced [aspect], a critical barrier has been
[gap]. Here, we [approach], which overcomes [barrier] by [key insight].

Specifically, we find that (i) [result 1 with number], (ii) [result 2
with number], and (iii) [result 3 with number]. Together, these
demonstrate [mechanism/principle] and enable [application].

We believe these findings are of broad interest to readers of
[Journal]: beyond [field], the principles extend to [adjacent domains].
This manuscript is posted as a preprint (bioRxiv: [DOI]) and is not
under consideration elsewhere. Suggested reviewers: [3 names +
affiliations]. Given potential conflicts, we respectfully request
exclusion of [1-2 names with reasons].

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Name, affiliation, ORCID, contact]
```
