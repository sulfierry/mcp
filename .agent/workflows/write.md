# Mega-Workflow: WRITE (Literature & Science Reading)

Use this workflow for deep scholarly research, paper structuring, literature mapping, and document generation.

### How to use:
This focuses on linguistic quality, scientific rigor, and proper academic formatting.

**Common Scenarios:**
- "Draft Chapter 2 of my Physics PhD thesis in LaTeX"
- "Conduct a Literature Review searching PubMed for DT-Kinase"
- "Summarize this PDF and extract methodology"
- "Format my references"

**Agentic Behavior:**
1. Routs to `thesis-writer`, `literature-review`, or other academic writers.
2. Performs data extraction from images, reads scholarly articles, and processes bibliographies.
3. Outputs robust markdown, LaTeX, or PDFs rather than executable application code.
