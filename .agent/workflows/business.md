# Mega-Workflow: BUSINESS (Corporate Strategy & Product)

Use this workflow to invoke the executive and growth branch of your agent capabilities.

### How to use:
This activates the "business brain" of the system, calling upon expert personas like Startup CTO, Marketing Director, and Growth Hacker.

**Common Scenarios:**
- "Create a Go-To-Market strategy for my new app"
- "Draft cold emails for lead generation"
- "Act as an experienced CTO and advise on technology stack choices"
- "Calculate market sizing and financial projections for Q3"

**Agentic Behavior:**
1. Employs C-level personas and strategic models.
2. Uses tools to calculate financial models or write persuasive marketing copy instead of hard logic code.
3. Will dynamically fetch missing skills from `alirezarezvani` repository if a niche executive persona is needed.
